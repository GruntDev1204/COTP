================================================================================
COTP (CHAOS-ONE-TIME-PROMPT) - DATA ARCHITECTURE & POOL SPECIFICATION
================================================================================

1. SYSTEM OVERVIEW & DATA FLOW ARCHITECTURE
The COTP application operates on a seedless combinatorial generation paradigm. 
Rather than relying on complex semantic inference models, the engine achieves 
astronomical output diversity by performing simultaneous, independent stochastic 
selections across discrete lexical and structural pools housed within the `data/` 
directory. To maximize memory efficiency and minimize runtime overhead, thematic 
sub-categories within large pools are organized via inline source comments rather 
than redundant sub-array allocations.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. MODULE-BY-MODULE DATA POOL SPECIFICATIONS (COTP COMPONENTS DATA POOL)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[Module: data.py] — Core Primitives, Numerical Modifiers & General Descriptors
    * clockPeriods: Defines diurnal time-of-day indicators (e.g., morning, noon, 
    night) appended to stochastically generated timestamps.
    * colors: Contains chromatic descriptors applied to architectural surfaces, 
    ambient lighting, and anomalous objects.
    * comment: Stores meta-commentary and concluding remarks appended to the terminal 
    segment of generated narratives.
    * dateEras: Specifies chronological era designators (e.g., BC/AD, Buddhist Era, 
    Holocene Era) utilized during historical date synthesis.
    * quantities: Provides quantifiers and numerical determiners preceding noun 
    phrases.
    * shapes: Defines geometric and morphological descriptors assigned to physical 
    and anomalous entities.
    * status: Contains general state adjectives and atmospheric descriptors used to 
    characterize scenes and conditions.
    * typeNumber: Supplies numerical suffixes (such as the imaginary unit 'i' or 
    empty strings) to deconstruct standard real-number constraints.
    * typeValueNumber: Supplies numerical prefixes (such as negative signs '-' or 
    empty strings) to enable negative and complex numerical generation.
    * units: Stores standard, scientific, and surreal units of measurement integrated 
    into quantitative statements.

[Module: dataIcon.py] — Unicode Emoji & Expressive Punctuation Pools
    * allIcons: The master aggregated pool of general-purpose emojis utilized for 
    visual ornamentation across generated prompts.
    * animalsNatureEmojis: A specialized subset containing zoological, botanical, 
    and meteorological icons.
    * emotionPunctuations: Expressive punctuation sequences appended to narrative 
    conclusions to modulate tone.
    * facePeopleEmojis: Anthropomorphic, facial expression, and character-based icons.
    * foodDrinkEmojis: Culinary, beverage, and consumable item icons.
    * objectsToolsEmojis: Artifact, instrument, and everyday utility icons.
    * sportsEntertainmentEmojis: Recreational, athletic, and artistic performance icons.
    * symbolsHeartsEmojis: Abstract symbols, geometric signs, and affective heart icons.
    * vehiclesPlacesEmojis: Transportation, infrastructure, and geographical icons.

[Module: dataIndoor.py] — Interior Spaces, Architectural Components & Lighting
    * indoorItemLightningSource: Defines external illumination sources (e.g., celestial 
    bodies, artificial beams) that project light from outside into an interior scene.
    * indoorItems: Represents fixed architectural apertures and structural components 
    (e.g., doors, windows, skylights, vents) through which external light enters 
    the interior space.
    * indoorItemStatus: Characterizes the physical condition of architectural 
    apertures (e.g., wide open, ajar, cracked, shattered).
    * indoorLightVerb: Specifies dynamic verbs describing how external illumination 
    penetrates, diffuses, or refracts through an `indoorItem` into the room.
    * indoorLocations: Defines enclosed architectural settings, rooms, and subterranean 
    complexes.
    * indoorMaterials: The master material pool used to describe interior structural 
    surfaces and construction compositions.
    * indoorMetalMaterials: A subset dedicated to metallic elements, alloys, and 
    industrial metals.
    * indoorNonMetalMaterials: A subset dedicated to organic, mineral, synthetic, and 
    non-metallic substances.
    * indoorObjectItems: Represents discrete, movable artifacts, machinery, and 
    anomalous items placed inside an interior environment.
    * inDoorPreposition: Spatial prepositions and locative phrases linking entities 
    to enclosed interior settings.
    * indoorStatus: Describes the ambient condition, atmosphere, or structural state 
    of an interior environment.

[Module: dataL4D2.py] — Tactical Simulation & Game Scenario Pools (Left 4 Dead 2)
    * charactersL4D2: Playable survivor character archetypes within the simulation.
    * grenadesL4D2: Tactical throwable ordnance and area-denial explosives.
    * mapsL4D2: Campaign environments, chapters, and combat theaters.
    * medicineL4D2: Medical supplies, restorative items, and temporary stimulants.
    * meleeL4D2: Close-quarters combat instruments and bladed/blunt weaponry.
    * pickupsL4D2: Deployable equipment, ammunition upgrades, and environmental pickups.
    * playerRulesL4D2: Behavioral constraints, challenge modifiers, and operational 
    rules governing player execution.
    * weaponsL4D2: Primary and secondary ballistic firearms across various tiers.

[Module: dataLove.py] — Interpersonal Dynamics & Romantic Prompt Synthesis
    * adjAppearanceGirls: Aesthetic and physical descriptors tailored for female 
    subjects.
    * adjApperanceBoys: Aesthetic and physical descriptors tailored for male subjects.
    * BoyActions: Behavioral actions and gestures performed by male subjects within 
    Couple Prompts.
    * coupleCall: Relational titles, terms of endearment, and pair designations.
    * fashionHairFemale: Hairstyle definitions for female subjects.
    * fashionHairMale: Hairstyle definitions for male subjects.
    * GirlActions: Behavioral actions and gestures performed by female subjects within 
    Couple Prompts.
    * LoveActions: Interactive, affectionate, and shared activities executed by the 
    couple.
    * loveIcons: Curated romantic and affective emojis dedicated to relationship-themed 
    outputs.
    * optionsDateApp: Simulated user interface choices and swipe actions for dating 
    application scenarios.

[Module: dataModelingPrompt.py] — Character Pose, Framing & Visual Composition
    * backgroundModeling: Scenic and environmental backdrop descriptions for character 
    portraits.

    * femaleArmPoses: Upper-limb positioning and hand placement directives for female

    * femaleExpressions: Facial affect and emotional expression descriptors for female 
    models.
    * femaleHair: Hair styling and length attributes for female modeling subjects.
    * femalePoses: Full-body posture definitions for female models, paired with boolean

    * femaleShoes: Footwear specifications for female models.
    * maleArmPoses: Upper-limb positioning and hand placement directives for male 
    models.
    * maleExpressions: Facial affect and emotional expression descriptors for male 
    models.
    * maleHair: Hair styling and length attributes for male modeling subjects.
    * malePoses: Full-body posture definitions for male models, paired with boolean 
    flags governing gaze direction compatibility.
    * maleShoes: Footwear specifications for male models.
    * modelFramings: Camera shot scales and cropping boundaries (e.g., full-body, 
    knee-up), coupled with metadata flags determining footwear visibility.
    * modelGazes: Ocular direction and visual engagement directives relative to the

[Module: dataNPC.py] — Autonomous Entities, Archetypes & Attributes
    * anyActions: Reusable action templates crossed with the shared anyObjects pool.
    * anyObjects: Shared nouns reused across absurd, conversational, and ceremonial
    action templates to avoid repeating the same object list in source code.
    * liveNPCActions: The full NPC action pool, including all anyActions × anyObjects
    combinations and the standalone feudal-ritual actions.
    * liveNPCApperances: Attire, physical traits, and visual styling descriptors for 
    NPCs.
    * liveNPCEmotions: Psychological states and emotional dispositions exhibited by 
    NPCs.
    * liveNPCs: The unified, high-cardinality pool of sentient beings, historical 
    figures, mythological deities, cryptids, kaiju, and anomalous entities.
    * nameNPC: Proper nouns and character identifiers assigned to generated entities.
    * NPCJobs: Occupational roles, feudal titles, and professional designations across 
    historical and modern eras.

[Module: dataOutdoor.py] — Exterior Landscapes, Celestial Phenomena & Weather
    * outdoorObjectDensity: Quantifiers and spatial distribution phrases describing 
    the prevalence of exterior objects.
    * outdoorObjects: Physical structures, landmarks, and anomalies situated within 
    open-air environments.
    * outDoorPreposition: Spatial prepositions linking subjects to exterior landscapes 
    and geographical features.
    * outDoorSkyObjects: Aerial entities, aircraft, and anomalies suspended in the sky.
    * outDoorSkyStrongObjects: Prominent celestial bodies and colossal aerial phenomena.
    * outDoorSkyStrongObjectStatus: Dynamic states and visual behaviors exhibited by 
    prominent celestial objects.
    * outDoorStatus: Environmental conditions and atmospheric descriptors for open-air 
    settings.
    * outdoorView: Macro-scale geographical landscapes, dimensional planes, and exterior 
    locations.
    * times: Temporal lighting phases across the diurnal cycle.
    * weatherConditions: Meteorological phenomena and atmospheric weather states.

[Module: dataStory.py] — Narrative World-Building, Lexicon & Transit
    * buildings: Architectural identifiers and structural designations used in narrative 
    generation.
    * businesses: Commercial establishments, enterprises, and institutional venues.
    * companies: Corporate entities and organizational names referenced in narratives 
    and dating simulations.
    • gpsLocations: Cardinal and relative directional vectors utilized in navigation
    segments.
    • LowerText: Lowercase alphabetic character pool for stochastic string assembly.
    • magicTypes: Supernatural abilities, arcane arts, and anomalous powers.
    • proverbs: Traditional adages and folk wisdom integrated into dialogue and lore.
    • slogan: Catchphrases, mottos, and promotional taglines.
    • sourceInfo: Information channels, media platforms, and epistemological sources
    cited within narratives.
    • UpperText: Uppercase alphabetic character pool used for initial capitalization
    in stochastic word generation.
    • Vehicle: Conveyances and transportation modes spanning non-motorized, terrestrial,
    aerial, and anomalous craft.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. SYSTEM CONFIGURATION CONSTANTS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• randomLargeNumberRange (Tuple): A boundary configuration tuple (min, max)
defining the lower and upper limits for macro-scale numerical generation within
generateANumber(). Unlike content arrays, this serves strictly as a parametric
range constraint.
